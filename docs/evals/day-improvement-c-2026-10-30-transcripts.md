## day-purchase-pause, turn 1

User: Teach me without onboarding. I want to buy a fancy phone because my friends upgraded. Explain needs versus wants using this example.

Agent: Need vs. want is about whether your life stops working without the thing. A phone that makes calls, texts, and lets you get to work or handle your bills is a need. A newer, pricier model that does the same jobs is a want, even when it's a great phone.

Here's the test: ask "If I don't buy this, what actually gets harder?" If the answer is "nothing I can't already do," it's a want. Your friends upgrading is a reason you feel the pull, not a reason the phone is a need.

Wants aren't bad. They're just the first thing to pay for after your needs, bills, and savings, which is why a pause before buying helps you check that the money is actually left over.
