## education-follow-up, turn 1

User: What is diversification? Explain in plain words, with a household example, without onboarding me.

Agent: Diversification means not putting all your money in one place, so one bad event can't wipe you out.

Think of a household that keeps its savings split between a bank account, a few index funds and some gold. If stocks fall one year, the gold may hold steady, and the bank savings stay the same. Their total drops less than it would if everything sat in stocks alone.

It doesn't stop losses. Assets can fall together in a crisis, and spreading money doesn't guarantee a gain. It only lowers the chance that one single problem hits everything at once.
